02 / Field briefing

Alleged puppy hazards

The Council's tongue-in-cheek risk register, followed by actual sensible safety advice.

FICTIONAL RISK · LOW

Unscheduled tail wagging

May distract staff from forms, meetings, or a very important sandwich. Recommended response: take a break.

FICTIONAL RISK · MODERATE

Compromised personal space

A puppy may attempt to sit on your shoe. Council policy recommends diplomatic negotiation and a gentle redirect.

REAL-WORLD NOTE

Nips, scratches & germs

Puppies can bite or scratch during play. Avoid roughhousing, wash hands after handling, and supervise children closely.

Actual public guidance

Be kind. Be calm. Ask first.

When meeting a real puppy, ask the owner or caregiver before approaching. Let the puppy come to you if comfortable, use gentle movements, and avoid hugging, cornering, or disturbing a sleeping or eating dog.

If a bite breaks the skin, wash the wound thoroughly with soap and running water and seek medical advice promptly, especially for deep wounds, bites to the face or hands, or uncertain rabies vaccination status.

Important distinctionThis satirical council does not recommend avoiding all puppies or treating them as a threat. Safe, respectful interactions are usually straightforward.
